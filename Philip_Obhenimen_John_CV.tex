\section{Experience}
\entry{Graduate Research Assistant}{\href{https://dmo-lab.com}{DMO Lab}, Louisiana State University, LA}{01/2022 - present}
Advisor: Dr.\ Ope Owoyele
\begin{itemize}
  \item Developed the \textbf{Liquid-Fueled Reactor Network (LFRN)}, to our knowledge the first coupled reactor-network
        framework for liquid-fueled combustors, adding spray breakup, droplet heating, and evaporation reactors; substantially
        reduced NO$_x$ error vs.\ gaseous networks and ran in $\mathcal{O}(10)$\,s on one core, \textbf{$\sim$2,300$\times$ faster}
        than 256-core CFD (first author, \textit{Combustion and Flame}).
  \item Trained \textbf{reinforcement-learning agents} that treat reactor-network construction as a sequential decision
        problem, clustering CFD fields automatically and improving lean-blowout prediction over hand-built networks
        (first author, \textit{Proceedings of the Combustion Institute}).
  \item Built \textbf{multi-fidelity Gaussian-process surrogates} (NARGP) fusing 400 reactor-network and 35 CFD runs to
        predict outlet temperature and six species; with only 7 CFD points, matched the accuracy a standard GP needs 14 for,
        lifting mean $R^2$ from 0.75 to 0.92, and identified where negative transfer occurs.
  \item Ran \textbf{RANS and large-eddy simulations (LES)} of swirl-stabilized, liquid-fueled combustors; RANS of a \textbf{Jet-A combustor in CONVERGE}: $k$-$\varepsilon$, flamelet-generated manifold
        chemistry with presumed $\beta$-PDF, Lagrangian sprays (TAB breakup, Frossling evaporation), and AMR to $\sim$3.2M cells;
        coupled networks to \textbf{Cantera} with HyChem Jet-A kinetics (119 species, 841 reactions).
  \item Developing a \textbf{diffusion model} that reconstructs high-resolution LES flow and species fields from
        reactor-network data (ongoing PhD project).
  \item Designed automated, parallel \textbf{HPC pipelines} on LSU's SuperMike-3 cluster (SLURM, MPI) linking simulation,
        data extraction, model construction, and ML training; trained \textbf{neural networks} for NO$_x$ prediction and built
        FGM tabulation pipelines with OpenSMOKE++.
\end{itemize}
