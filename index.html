01 — PhD Research · DMO Lab, LSU

From high-fidelity CFD to real-time combustion models

CFD is the most trusted way to predict combustor emissions, but each operating point costs thousands of CPU-hours. My dissertation builds a pipeline that keeps the physics and drops the cost.

  1. 01

    High-fidelity simulation

    Combustion CFD

    RANS and LES of swirl-stabilized, liquid-fueled gas-turbine combustors with Lagrangian sprays, tabulated chemistry, and adaptive mesh refinement on HPC.

    ~3.2M cells · 10 h on 256 cores

  2. 02

    Reduced-order modeling

    Reactor networks

    Clustering turns millions of CFD cells into a handful of chemical reactors with detailed Jet-A kinetics, including custom spray-evaporation reactors I developed.

    O(10) s on 1 core · >1,000× faster

  3. 03

    Machine & deep learning

    ML-augmented models

    RL agents build the networks automatically, multi-fidelity Gaussian processes fuse cheap network data with scarce CFD, and neural networks predict NOx.

    CFD accuracy with half the CFD runs

  4. 04

    Generative AI

    Diffusion models

    A diffusion model that reconstructs high-resolution LES flow and species fields from reactor-network output, one of the final projects of my PhD.

02 — Expertise

What I do, and the proof

Every skill here is backed by peer-reviewed research or shipped software.

Primary focus

Combustion CFD & Reduced-Order Modeling

I simulate multiphase turbulent reacting flows and compress them into reduced-order models fast enough for emissions prediction and design-space exploration.

  • Liquid-Fueled Reactor Network: to our knowledge, the first coupled reactor-network framework for liquid-fueled combustors, with reactors for spray breakup, droplet heating, and evaporation. It was validated against CFD across inlet temperatures of 300–500 K and a range of fuel flow rates (Combustion and Flame).
  • Combustor CFD in CONVERGE: RANS k-ε, flamelet-generated manifold chemistry with a presumed β-PDF, Lagrangian sprays (TAB breakup, Frossling evaporation), and AMR.
  • Detailed kinetics: Cantera with the HyChem Jet-A mechanism (119 species, 841 reactions), resolving thermal, prompt, and N2O-pathway NOx.
